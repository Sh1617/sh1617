## 🎓 IIT Madras — Software Engineering Project

### Life Skills App for School-Aged Children

A collaborative software engineering project based on a problem statement provided by the **Indian Institute of Technology Madras (IIT Madras)**.

Our team consisted of **7 members** and competed with more than **50 participating teams**.

<details>
<summary><b>🏆 Our Achievement — Click to Read</b></summary>

Our team secured **4th Rank among 50+ teams** for the project and received a **Certificate of Appreciation**.

This was a collaborative achievement involving teamwork, software development, problem-solving, and implementation of the assigned project.

</details>

### Project Overview

An AI-powered platform designed to support life skills development, learning habits, emotional awareness, and personal growth among school-aged children.

**Key Features**

- 🧠 AI-powered mood classification.
- 📚 AI-generated stories and quizzes.
- 📰 Child-friendly news and infotainment.
- 📊 Activity reports and progress tracking.
- 🎯 Tasks, achievements, streaks, and badges.
- 👨‍👩‍👧 Parent dashboard and child activity insights.
